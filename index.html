A residential vision in Sector 150

The project

A New Perspective on Residential Living.

Prestige Sector 150 introduces a residential development by Prestige Group in Noida's Sector 150 Sports City. Planned across approximately 14.83 acres*, the development is envisioned with around 900 residences* in 2, 3 and 4 BHK configurations.

Set within one of Noida's designated Sports City districts, the address offers an opportunity to experience a more open residential environment, complemented by landscaped spaces and proposed lifestyle amenities.
