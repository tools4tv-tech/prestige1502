A residential vision in Sector 150

The project

A New Perspective on Residential Living.

Prestige Sector 150 introduces a residential development by Prestige Group in Noida's Sector 150 Sports City. Planned across approximately 14.8 acres*, the development is envisioned with around 1,025 residences* in 2, 3 and 4 BHK configurations, set across 12 towers rising G+18 to G+22 floors* over two basement levels.

Set within Noida's Sports City district, the address offers low-density, green living close to the 43-acre Shaheed Bhagat Singh Park, complemented by landscaped spaces, proposed lifestyle amenities and an international-level tennis academy and stadium.

Frequently asked questions

Everything You May Want to Know.

Information currently available is preliminary. We recommend independently confirming the latest official project disclosures before making a decision.

The proposed residential development is located in Sector 150, Noida, within the Sports City district, with direct access to the Noida–Greater Noida Expressway (about 2 km) and the Sector 148 Aqua Line metro station about 3 km away.

The project is associated with Prestige Group (Prestige Estates Projects Limited). Verify project association and current disclosures directly with the developer.

The current pre-launch information lists five typologies: 2 BHK + 2T, 3 BHK + 2T, 3 BHK + 3T, 3 BHK + 3T + maid's room, and 4 BHK + 4T + maid's room.

About 1,025 residences are planned across 12 towers on a 14.8-acre land parcel. The towers rise G+18 to G+22 floors, with two levels of basement. All figures are indicative and subject to official confirmation.

Indicative base prices start at ₹2.5 Cr* for the 2 BHK and go up to ₹6.6 Cr* for the 4 BHK, with a basic sale price of approx. ₹20,000 per sq. ft. Other charges and taxes are additional. Prices are subject to change; ask for the latest official cost sheet.

The current pre-launch information lists a construction-linked payment plan. Ask for the official payment schedule before making any commitment.

Indicative super areas are 1,250–1,450 sq. ft. for 2 BHK, 1,550–2,580 sq. ft. for the 3 BHK variants and 3,250–3,300 sq. ft. for 4 BHK. All are subject to official confirmation.

UP RERA registration is under process according to the current pre-launch information. No project registration number has been issued in that information. Verify the latest status directly with UP RERA before making any commitment.

Indicative 3 BHK and 4 BHK floor plans are shown in the Residences section, and the 2 BHK layout is available on request. These layouts are indicative and may change; confirm them against RERA-registered documents when available.

The proposed facilities include an international-level tennis academy and stadium, padel, pickleball and basketball courts, a swimming pool, outdoor gym, skating area, bicycle lane, amphitheatre, yoga lawn, tree house, sand pit and pet park, alongside a clubhouse, gymnasium, indoor sports hall and family recreation areas. Final provisions are subject to official project documents.

Sector 150 is planned as a Sports City with low-density, green living and the 43-acre Shaheed Bhagat Singh Park. It has direct expressway access, a nearby Aqua Line metro station and proximity to the upcoming Noida International Airport, IT hubs such as Microsoft, TCS, Infosys and Candor Tech Park, and Knowledge Park in Greater Noida. The sector is planned with no overhead wires, water supply from the Ganga, a proper sewage system, and hospitals, schools and retail within the sector.
