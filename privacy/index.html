Privacy Policy

This page describes the current behavior of this demonstration website. It is an informational and promotional page for the proposed Prestige Sector 150 Noida development and is not the official website of Prestige Group. The operator's verified legal identity and contact details should be added before public launch.

Enquiry form

The enquiry form currently runs in demonstration mode. It validates form entries in your browser but does not send, save, or share your personal information. A secure server-side endpoint and a verified privacy notice must be configured before live lead collection begins.

Information you enter

The form requests your name and Indian mobile number, with an optional email address, enquiry category, project name, page URL and communication consent. Do not submit personal information until the website operator has connected and documented its secure processing and retention practices.

Cookies and analytics

This static demonstration does not intentionally set analytics or advertising cookies. Hosting providers, embedded services or future integrations may process technical data according to their own practices. Review and update this policy if analytics, advertising, CRM or other third-party services are added.

Your choices

Because the current demo does not transmit or store form submissions, it has no lead record to access or delete. For a production website, publish a verified privacy contact and explain how people can exercise applicable rights or withdraw communication consent.

Updates

This policy should be reviewed by the website operator and amended to reflect actual data flows before the site is used to collect live enquiries.
